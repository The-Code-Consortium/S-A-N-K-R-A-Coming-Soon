About SANKĀRA

An unhurried atmosphere away from city noise.

SANKĀRA is an exclusive, intimate boutique hotel located along Ampitiya Road in the cultural center of Kandy, Sri Lanka. Built on years of patience, architectural precision, and uncompromising craftsmanship, SANKĀRA offers a serene sanctuary designed for refined comfort and well-being.

Our property combines clean, minimalist architecture with rich, natural materials featuring our signature 4×4 checkered wood motifs, deep walnut tones, brushed brass, and natural linens complemented by traditional Sri Lankan art accents. Designed to offer an unhurried atmosphere away from city noise, SANKĀRA is located just minutes from Kandy Lake.
